### Hi, I'm Marijn

Engineering leader from Ghent, Belgium. Husband, father of a seven-year-old, long-distance runner.
That's about it, in a nutshell.

By day I build great things with the engineering team at [Urbantz](https://www.urbantz.com). By
night (well, evening) I run a lot and build more things for myself and for my wife's
[Toddle & Tumble](https://www.toddletumble.be). The side projects live at
[Small Victories](https://www.small-victories.co): mostly apps for kids, born from our own needs as
a family, plus a few web tools and some open source. Everything is on the
[projects page](https://www.small-victories.co/projects).

> "In the long run, it's just a bunch of short runs"

**Apps** (App Store and Google Play)

All of them are made for kids, and I'm proud that none of them track anything: no ads, no
analytics.

- [Rilo](https://www.small-victories.co/projects/rilo): the most mature one. A first phone for young
  children that only reaches the people their parents approve. Safe and end-to-end encrypted.
- [Brush Buddy](https://www.small-victories.co/projects/brush-buddy): a two-minute brushing timer
  that gets kids to brush all their teeth, not just one spot.
- [Kiddypaint](https://www.small-victories.co/projects/kidypaint): turns any photo into a
  colour-by-number page. Simple, so don't expect too much of it. Your photos stay on the phone.
- [Math Adventure](https://www.small-victories.co/projects/math-adventure): a calm, offline maths
  game for ages 6 to 9.

**Fun web apps and handy tools**

- [Pass the Aux](https://www.small-victories.co/projects/pass-the-aux): a DJ booth for a party with
  friends, where every guest can suggest the next track
  ([code](https://github.com/marijnvandevoorde/pass-the-aux)).
- [Photoboot](https://www.small-victories.co/projects/photoboot): a party photo booth in the browser
  that prints stickers on a Bluetooth thermal printer
  ([code](https://github.com/marijnvandevoorde/photoboot)).
- [SEPA QR](https://www.small-victories.co/projects/sepa-qr): payment QR codes, made entirely in
  your browser ([code](https://github.com/marijnvandevoorde/sepaqr)).
- [The best Chrome extension in the world](https://www.small-victories.co/projects/the-best-chrome-extension-in-the-world):
  maybe the one I'm proudest of. Elevator music for Google Meet, and a background in the colour of
  your shirt.
- [google-calendar-sync](https://github.com/marijnvandevoorde/google-calendar-sync): an Apps Script
  that keeps several Google calendars in sync across accounts.
